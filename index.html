The approach

A connection is a starting point.
Evidence determines what follows.

Our development focus is a clearer investigation workspace for quality, reliability and failure-analysis professionals—built around three principles.

01 / PRESERVE

Keep evidence in context.

Retain the source, its limitations and the distinction between an observation and an interpretation.

02 / CONNECT

Explore relationships.

Examine shared components, suppliers, processes and conditions while keeping unrelated explanations open.

03 / CHALLENGE

Ask what could disprove it.

Make uncertainty, contradictions and missing evidence visible before reaching a conclusion.

Evidence before certainty. Causivyn is in development. Demonstrations use synthetic cases; production readiness and real-world investigative performance have not been established.
